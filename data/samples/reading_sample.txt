Today I focused on understanding task resumption in long-running software projects.
I reviewed several recent research papers discussing cognitive load and how developers
struggle to regain context after interruptions.

I noticed that traditional to-do lists do not capture enough mental context,
especially for complex research tasks that span multiple days.

I also explored whether large language models could help reconstruct
a developer’s mental state using partial activity logs and notes.

Finally, I compared popular abstractive summarization models such as
BART, T5, and PEGASUS to see which might be suitable for this project.
Due to limited compute resources, I am leaning toward using BART-Base
for the thin vertical slice of the system.
